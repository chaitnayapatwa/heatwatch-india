ILLUSTRATIVE COMPOSITE INDEX

Risk = 0.35H + 0.25E + 0.20V + 0.20(100 − A)

H = heat intensity, E = exposure, V = vulnerability, A = adaptive capacity. Values are normalised to 0–100 for this student-project demonstration.
